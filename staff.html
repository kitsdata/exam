Before you sign in
  1. Close every browser window, then open a fresh private window: Chrome → New Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N), Edge → New InPrivate window (Ctrl+Shift+N) or Firefox → New private window (Ctrl+Shift+P).
  2. Sign in only with your @karunya.edu staff account. Student accounts (@karunya.edu.in) cannot open the staff pages.
  3. If you see "Sorry, unable to open the file", another Google / Gmail account is signed in: close all private windows and start again with a fresh one.
